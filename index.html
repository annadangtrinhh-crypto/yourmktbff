How I think about marketing

01

Get people interested

A good idea still needs to be noticed.

I like thinking about the audience, the message, the content, and the channel, and finding a way to make a product or idea easier to understand, remember, and care about.

02

Turn interest into action

A click is nice. A conversation, inquiry, or sale tells you more.

I look at the journey from the first impression to the final action and try to understand what helps people move forward, and what gets in the way.

03

Test, don't just guess

Some of my favourite projects started with a simple question: "What happens if I try this?"

I enjoy putting ideas into the real world, watching what happens, and adjusting along the way. My own e-commerce experiments taught me that sometimes the most useful answer comes from actually testing the idea.

04

Learn from the result

Not everything works the first time. That's part of the process.

For me, marketing is about taking what happened (the numbers, customer reactions, conversations, and unexpected results) and using it to make the next decision a little smarter.

"Okay… your idea actually worked." 💬

That's the kind of reaction I like working toward.

Not because every idea needs to be a huge success, but because good work should move something forward.
